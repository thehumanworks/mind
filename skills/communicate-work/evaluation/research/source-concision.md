# Concision, audience, essential meaning, and information volume

Research notes for the parent agent. Retrieved 2026-10-21. Only the 18 supplied URLs are used as evidence. Search results outside that exact allowlist were ignored. These are paraphrased source findings, not a drafted skill. No evaluations were run.

## Core conclusions

1. Define the reader's task and the communication's purpose before selecting details. The same topic needs different treatment for an expert, a novice, a decision maker, or someone who must act.
2. Judge concision by useful meaning per unit of attention, not an absolute word limit. Keep enough context, evidence, nuance, and instructions for the reader to understand and act.
3. Usually answer the actual question or state the conclusion first, then add essential support. Do not automatically reproduce the order in which the writer discovered information.
4. Remove duplication and language that does no work. Preserve words that encode genuine uncertainty, useful diplomacy, a necessary distinction, or essential evidence.
5. Stop once the current purpose is fulfilled. Extra paraphrases, examples proving the same thing, and answers to every imaginable follow-up can burden the reader. A complex request can still justify a long answer.

These conclusions are a synthesis of the evidence below. The individual sources do not establish universal response-length thresholds or validated AI-agent behavior rules.

## Adaptation cautions for the parent agent

- Prefer the recurring, qualified principles over mechanical quotas. Some sources offer sentence counts or approximate word targets; other passages explicitly allow needed length and complexity.
- Concision examples are not always semantically safe. Talaera's status rewrite removes the original approximation around the completion percentage and adds a Friday deadline absent from the original. Do not copy that as a meaning-preserving transformation. This is an observation about that example, not a recommendation from the source (`turn22view0`, lines 143–146).
- The English Center's treatment of uncertainty is a useful safeguard against applying generic advice to remove hedges indiscriminately. A qualifier is removable only if the intended meaning and confidence remain intact.
- Distinguish a source's communication advice from an empirical claim. These pages do not collectively prove that a particular maximum length, fixed number of bullets, or response template is optimal for AI agents.
- Root citation requirement: before citing a subagent retrieval reference in the user-facing answer, independently open the allowed URL or search/find it, as required by the web-tool instructions.
