## Synthesis boundaries

- **Detail is conditional.** Strapi emphasizes missing prerequisites; Microsoft allows assumed programming fundamentals; INNOQ recommends omitting irrelevant or already obvious material. A defensible combined rule is to preserve what this reader needs to understand or act and omit what does not serve that need.
- **Do not turn source-specific heuristics into universal quotas.** MDN offers a sentence-length heuristic; Mintlify offers paragraph and onboarding-time heuristics. These are not evidence for a mandatory response length or fixed number of sections, bullets, examples, or words.
- **Tone varies by genre.** Google's conversational guidance and Strapi's neutrality rule are house styles. Google's preference for direct address also differs from Strapi's limited-pronoun policy. Use clarity and context as the common rule.
- **Diátaxis is a useful intention check.** Applying its four purposes to agent replies is a synthesis, not a direct instruction in the source to force every chat message into a four-part document.
- **Completeness is scoped.** A concise answer can be complete for its question. A reference requires fuller coverage of its declared subject. Removing essential conditions, failure cases, or requested reasoning is not justified by brevity advice.
- **Source specificity matters.** GitLab-specific markup, UI shortcodes, and docs-first contribution rules are implementation conventions, not general communication requirements. No recommendation here authorizes messages, publication, or other external actions.
