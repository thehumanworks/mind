## Keep the frameworks distinct

- **BLUF:** Put the information the reader most needs to know or act on at the beginning. Wikipedia provides a useful cross-check but has an editorial-quality notice; stronger claims should use the named practical sources. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BLUF_(communication)) genui{"citation":{"ref":"turn4view1"}}
- **Pyramid Principle:** Structure support beneath a governing conclusion, with coherent grouping and ordering. It adds a logic hierarchy beyond merely moving a conclusion upward. [StrategyU](https://strategyu.co/pyramid-principle-partone/) genui{"citation":{"ref":"turn11view1"}}
- **Inverted pyramid:** Order the conclusion and facts by importance, followed by background, so partial reading remains useful. This is not identical to a hierarchy of arguments. [NN/G](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/inverted-pyramids-in-cyberspace/) genui{"citation":{"ref":"turn5view3"}}
- **SCQA:** A possible introduction that establishes shared situation, complication, and question before the answer. The summaries allow flexibility and short context; it should not become a mandatory preamble to an already clear question. [To Summarise](https://www.tosummarise.com/book-summary-the-pyramid-principle-by-barbara-minto/) genui{"citation":{"ref":"turn19view3"}}

FourWeekMBA explicitly separates these structures. LogRocket treats BLUF and the Minto method as interchangeable; retain the useful common pattern without adopting that historical or terminological equivalence. [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/bluf-bottom-line-up-front/) and [LogRocket](https://blog.logrocket.com/product-management/using-the-bluf-acronym-to-improve-communication/) genui{"citation":{"refs":["turn12view0","turn4view3"]}}

## Source-specific conventions and cautions

- **No universal numerical cap emerges.** FourWeekMBA, Umbrex, LegalClarity, and StrategyCase offer different counts of sentences, reasons, and supporting points. Those are writing heuristics in particular formats, not evidence for a fixed AI-answer word limit. Do not convert them into one.
- **Do not import old web statistics.** NN/G's 1997 article gives screen-reading and text-reduction percentages in an early-monitor context. Its 1996 article includes a 2003 correction explicitly withdrawing the broad claim that users do not scroll. The durable recommendation is to prioritize and make content scannable, not repeat those historical claims as current facts. genui{"citation":{"refs":["turn5view2","turn5view3"]}}
- **Do not turn editorial language into mandatory labels.** Matt Ström-Awn says adding a BLUF heading does not itself produce clarity. Shortform explicitly invents names such as “Peak Conclusion” for its explanation; those are its terminology. genui{"citation":{"refs":["turn11view0","turn11view3"]}}
- **Do not remove truthful uncertainty.** Codeless suggests trimming words such as “maybe” and “potentially.” Apply that only to empty hesitation: deleting a factual qualifier can change the claim. This safeguard is our inference from fidelity, not an explicit Codeless rule. genui{"citation":{"ref":"turn19view0"}}
- **Do not require artificial choices or conceal unmitigated risk.** River's exact three-option template, preference-anchoring rationale, fixed page limits, and absolute instruction to pair every risk with a mitigation are its conventions. A compact agent skill can retain fair comparison and material risk disclosure without forcing those mechanics. genui{"citation":{"refs":["turn5view1","turn12view3"]}}
- **Do not promote cognitive numerology to a fact.** StrategyU challenges the claim that people naturally process all information as pyramids and the familiar working-memory count. To Summarise also flags disagreement about that count. Grouping is useful writing advice here, not proof of a universal brain limit. genui{"citation":{"refs":["turn11view1","turn13view2"]}}
- **Do not require exhaustive formality in every answer.** MECE, specific logical taxonomies, and SCQA come from consulting/document structures. Their transferable aim is coherent, nonredundant support without material gaps. Source summaries differ even in how they enumerate the ordering methods. genui{"citation":{"refs":["turn19view2","turn13view2"]}}
- **Military provenance is indirectly supported here.** Several pages quote or describe military guidance. The exact allowed handbook URL failed, so do not claim firsthand verification of the handbook itself or follow its out-of-allowlist links.
