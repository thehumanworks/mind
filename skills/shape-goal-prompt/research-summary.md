# Research synthesis: from a rough request to an executable goal

The skill is a practical synthesis of requirements engineering, human–AI interaction research, clarification studies, and current agent guidance. No source establishes a universally optimal prompt template, a fixed number of questions, or guaranteed long-task success.

## Design decisions

**Make the goal and evidence explicit.** NASA's requirements guidance supports clear, feasible, traceable requirements and suitable ways to check them. Its validation guidance separately asks whether the result serves the intended use. Applied here, a coherent prompt must both preserve the user's intended outcome and specify evidence for the resulting work. Numeric targets are useful only when their meaning and basis are defensible; inspection or a review can be the appropriate acceptance method. [NASA requirements](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/appendix-c-how-to-write-a-good-requirement/) · [validation](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/5-4-product-validation/)

**Spend attention on consequential uncertainty.** Mixed-initiative interaction research considers the value of clarification alongside interruption cost. Grounding theory treats understanding as sufficient for the current purpose and repairable through interaction. The resulting skill asks about choices that change the assignment, gives concrete alternatives or a useful draft, and incorporates targeted corrections. This is an application of those principles, not a tested universal dialogue policy. [Horvitz](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/principles-mixed-initiative-user-interfaces/) · [Clark and Brennan](https://web.stanford.edu/~clark/1990s/Clark%2C%20H.H.%20_%20Brennan%2C%20S.E.%20_Grounding%20in%20communication_%201991.pdf)

**Evaluate clarification and the handoff separately.** IN3 is a close precedent: an interaction component elicits missing intent and summarizes an actionable goal before execution. Its trained components and simulated conversations differ from a reusable instruction skill. ClarifyGPT adds evidence from executable programming tasks, but its small benchmark tasks cannot establish broad long-horizon effectiveness. These studies motivate testing information preservation and downstream artifacts, beyond how polished a prompt looks. [IN3](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.61/) · [ClarifyGPT manuscript](https://www.eecs.yorku.ca/~wangsong/papers/fse24.pdf)

**Maintain a corrected current specification.** Multi-turn instruction experiments found failures associated with early assumptions and later consolidation. The skill therefore replaces superseded decisions while retaining unrelated requirements, then produces a standalone handoff. The study's simulated conversations and historical models do not establish a numerical prediction for this skill. [Laban et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.06120v1)

**Be precise about results and flexible about methods.** OpenAI's reasoning guidance emphasizes clear targets and constraints. Anthropic's context guidance seeks enough specificity without brittle procedural logic. Both support a brief that carries useful context and evidence requirements while leaving room for execution judgment. Explicit user methods still remain constraints. [OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning-best-practices) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents)

**Treat continuity as an environment capability.** The Codex Goals guide connects objectives to verification, boundaries and a blocked stop condition. Anthropic's long-running experiments use external state and verification, but later remove structures that become unnecessary for more capable models. The skill makes progress records and checkpoints conditional, without assuming a prompt creates storage, tools or repeated sessions. [Codex Goals](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/codex/using_goals_in_codex) · [Anthropic harness work](https://www.anthropic.com/research/harness-design-long-running-apps)

**Optimize within the user's objective.** Goal-oriented requirements research distinguishes goals, constraints, preferences and alternative refinements. The skill applies that distinction by separating required success from optional improvements, preserving resource and authorization limits, and refusing to redefine success around convenient evidence. It does not promise a global optimum. [van Lamsweerde](https://webperso.info.ucl.ac.be/~avl/files/RE01.pdf)

## Evidence limits and evaluation response

Official guidance is not a controlled trial of this skill. Research systems often involve training, particular models and tools, or unusually cooperative simulated users. Our evaluation therefore preserves raw outputs, compares independent candidates and a control, tests multi-turn corrections and clear-input cases, and includes small downstream artifact checks. Blinded model grading reduces some bias but does not replace human validation; scripted feedback tests incorporation more directly than question-selection effectiveness. [OpenAI evals](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices) · [information-gain clarification](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/deng26l.html)
